Founder, RAJ FASHION

I started RAJ FASHION in 2026 from our home area in B.K. Nagar, Jajira. My cousin Sabbir used to say good clothes in our town cost too much or looked too plain. I wanted a shop where a student and a bridegroom could both find something worth wearing.

I still pick every design myself and check each piece before it goes into the parcel. When a customer messages me at night asking about a panjabi for tomorrow, I answer. That is the whole business.
